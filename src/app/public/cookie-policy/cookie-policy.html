1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser when you visit QuizPlay. They help us keep the platform secure, remember your preferences, and improve performance.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential cookies: required for login, session handling, and security.
  • Preference cookies: remember choices like theme or language settings.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand product usage and improve learning flows.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services used in QuizPlay (for analytics or embedded content) may set their own cookies. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can clear or block cookies from your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to sign in or use key platform features.

5. Contact

For questions about our cookie usage, contact us at support@quizplay.co.in.
